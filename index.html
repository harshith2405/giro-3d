How to Use the Viewer

Step-by-step interactive tool instructions
📈 Path Profile & Elevation
1 Click Path Profile on the left icon dock.
2 Click two points on the 3D terrain to create the measurement path.
3 Drag yellow spherical handles to dynamically refine path endpoints.
4 Click the graph in the right info panel to expand, or click Save.
💡 Pro Tip: The 3D line drapes directly over ground hills and dips following actual DEM elevations.
🧊 Volume & Cut/Fill Volumetrics
1 Click Volume Analysis on the left icon dock.
2 Click across terrain to place polygon boundary vertices.
3 Double-click or click the starting vertex to close the boundary.
4 Review real-time Pit Cut Volume & Stockpile Fill Volume in the Right Info Panel.
💡 Pro Tip: Click Save Volume Area to store with custom folder tags on the cloud.
✂️ ROI (Region of Interest) Clipping
1 Click ROI on the right toolbar to choose Box, Poly, or KML.
2 Box Mode: Click and drag a 2D bounding rectangle across the map.
3 Poly / KML Mode: Draw multi-point polygon boundaries or upload `.kml`.
4 Click Reset ROI anytime to restore full site terrain extents.
💡 Pro Tip: Clipping an ROI dramatically speeds up rendering on massive mine sites!
🎛️ Analytical Overlays & Shaders
1 Elevations Grid (#): Live elevation altitude numbers across visible terrain.
2 Slope Heatmap: Color gradient terrain steepness analysis with Haul Road safety thresholds (0–10°).
3 Topographic Contours: Dynamic contour line shader with Mining, Survey, and Overview presets.
💡 Pro Tip: All layer settings can be customized in real time in the Right Info Panel!
🎮 3D Camera Navigation & Hotkeys
🖱️ Left Click + Drag: Orbit and rotate the 3D terrain freely.
✋ Right Click + Drag: Pan / slide across the site.
🔍 Scroll Wheel: Smooth zoom in and out.
⌨️ Arrow Keys: Continuous smooth inertia fly-through gliding.
💡 Pro Tip: Use the bottom-right camera widget to quickly toggle 2D, 2.5D, and 3D views.
